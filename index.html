Game Rules

  • Layout: 5 reels × 3 rows, up to 20 lines.
  • Total Bet = Lines × Bet per Line.
  • Visual symbols are decorative; outcome is determined by the line‑chance engine.
  • On any paid spin that wins: your stake for that spin is refunded first, then the win amount is added.
  • Every 10 paid spins: 1 Free Spin. Also 40% chance for a Lucky Bonus on that spin (capped by 3× total bet).
  • Single‑spin cap: win is limited to 3× Total Bet.
  • House balance: dynamic RTP aims around ~70% baseline (auto‑adjusts a bit if your balance is very low/high).
