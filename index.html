Game Rules

  • Layout: 5 reels × 3 rows, up to 20 lines.
  • Total Bet = Lines × Bet per Line.
  • Line wins pay left to right on active lines. Wild substitutes for all symbols except Scatter and Bonus.
  • Scatter pays anywhere. Payout for 3/4/5 scatters is based on Bet per Line (not lines).
  • Bonus: 3+ Bonus symbols award Free Spins.
  • On any paid spin that wins, your stake for that spin is refunded first, then the win amount is added.
  • Single‑spin cap: win is limited to 3× Total Bet.
  • Target long‑term RTP ≈ 84% (tunable). Short‑term results may vary.
